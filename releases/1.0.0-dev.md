## Approved Version 1.0 Functional Scope

The following administrative capabilities are approved for Version 1.0.

### Payroll Period Management

Version 1.0 will add managed payroll-period records with start and end dates,
draft and finalized states, audit history, and safe removal controls.

Draft periods may be permanently deleted after confirmation. Finalized periods
will be voided or archived instead of silently erased. Removing a
payroll-period record will not delete the underlying employee punches.

The administrator confirmation screen will identify the period dates, status,
employee count, punch count, and report-artifact tracking status before any
destructive action is accepted. CSV and PDF reports are generated on demand
and are not retained as payroll-period-linked artifacts.

### Supervisor User Management

Version 1.0 will add an administrator-only interface for creating and managing
supervisor accounts.

Administrators will be able to create supervisors, edit account information,
reset passwords, activate or deactivate accounts, review last-login
information, and manage authorized role assignments.

Supervisor accounts with operational or audit history will normally be
deactivated rather than permanently deleted. The system will protect the final
active administrator account and prevent accidental administrator lockout.

All important payroll-period and supervisor-account actions will be recorded
through the audit framework.
